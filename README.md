# Glasshour

Clear aligner wear timer for iPhone · 隐形牙套佩戴计时

One tap when your aligners come out, one tap when they go back in. Glasshour counts down today’s out time, shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and rings an alarm—even on Silent—when time is up.

- Website: https://franklioxygen.github.io/glasshour-app/
- Privacy Policy: https://franklioxygen.github.io/glasshour-app/privacy.html
- Support: https://franklioxygen.github.io/glasshour-app/support.html

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/franklioxygen/glasshour-app/issues).

The site is plain HTML served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch; the pages are available in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français and Deutsch.
